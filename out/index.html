Product range

Built for industrial formulation

Cacao Beans

Fermented and sun-dried beans graded to international standards, available in bulk container volumes.

  • Moisture: 6-7%
  • Fat content: 50-57%
  • Grade: I & II

Cacao Powder

Natural and alkalized cacao powder milled to precise particle sizes for industrial food applications.

  • Fat content: 10-12% / 20-22%
  • pH: 5.3-7.5
  • Mesh: 200-325

Cacao Butter

Deodorized and natural cacao butter, press-extracted for confectionery and chocolate manufacturing.

  • Free fatty acid: <1.75%
  • Melting point: 32-35°C
  • Food-grade packaging

Cacao Liquor / Mass

100% pure ground cacao mass, block or chip format, ready for large-scale chocolate production lines.

  • Fat content: 54-58%
  • Fineness: <30 microns
  • Custom block sizing
